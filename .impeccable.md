## Design Context

### Users
Retail Deriv traders (18+, mostly South Africa) who run a last-digit pattern bot on demo or real accounts, on laptop and as an iPhone PWA. They want to set rules, start the bot, and check results quickly.

### Brand Personality
Precise, honest, calm. Never hypey. Fintech clarity without marketing claims: no guarantees, no "risk-free", no fake numbers.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light "cool paper" fintech with a tinted teal-dark mode. Space Grotesk display, JetBrains Mono for data. Soft surfaces, thin borders, floating UI mockups labelled as illustrative. Motion: staggered reveals, gentle floats, lift-on-hover. Plain ASCII text only (no special typographic symbols).

### Design Principles
1. Show the product, not stock imagery.
2. Every claim must be true of the software.
3. Risk warning is always visible, never hidden.
4. Motion conveys entry and depth; never bounce.
5. One accent (primary teal); bull/bear only for P/L.
